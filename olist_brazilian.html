Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Analysis

Goal

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset using SQL to uncover business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, seller performance, customer retention, and product quality.

13. Delivery time vs. review score

Question: Does faster delivery correlate with a higher review score?

SQL: 12_correlation_delivery_review_score.sql

Finding: The Pearson correlation coefficient between delivery time and review score is -0.334, indicating a weak negative correlation.This suggests that customers who receive their orders more quickly tend to give slightly higher review scores, although delivery speed alone does not strongly predict customer ratings.

VII. Order Analysis

14. Installment payment behavior

Question: Do orders paid in more installments have a higher average order value?

SQL: 14_customers_with_installments.sql

Finding: Customers who use installment payments spend more than customers who pay without installments. Customers using a higher number of installments also tend to spend more.
