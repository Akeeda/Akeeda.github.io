Bike Share Analysis Dashboard

Business Task

Cyclistic's marketing team wants to understand how annual members and casual riders use the bike-share service differently. The goal of this analysis is to identify behavioral differences between the two rider groups and provide recommendations that could help convert casual riders into annual members.

Data Sources

  • Cyclistic/Divvy historical trip data covering 12 months.
  • Data includes ride information such as rider type, ride duration, start station, date, and time of ride.
  • Data was obtained from the Google Data Analytics Capstone Case Study dataset.
  • The analysis focuses on comparing casual riders and annual members.
  • Dataset obtained from:View Original Dataset

Data Cleaning & Manipulation

  • Combined 12 months of trip data into a single dataset.
  • Removed duplicate records.
  • Removed records with missing or invalid values.
  • Calculated ride duration.
  • Extracted month, weekday, and hour from ride timestamps.
  • Created weekday/weekend classifications.
  • Created peak-hour analysis fields.
  • Prepared aggregated datasets for Tableau visualizations.

Summary of Analysis

Analysis of 12 months of Cyclistic trip data revealed clear behavioral differences between annual members and casual riders. Members primarily use bikes for weekday commuting and exhibit consistent usage throughout the year, while casual riders are more active on weekends and tend to take longer recreational trips. These findings suggest opportunities to convert casual riders into annual members through targeted marketing and membership incentives.

Project Outcome

This analysis identified key behavioral differences between annual members and casual riders. The findings support targeted marketing strategies aimed at increasing annual membership adoption among casual riders through digital campaigns, membership incentives, and commuter-focused promotions.
