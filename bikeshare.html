🚲 Bike Share Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes bike share usage trends using Tableau to uncover rider behavior, commuting patterns, station demand, and operational insights.

The dashboard was designed to support data-driven decision-making through interactive visual storytelling.

🎯 Business Objectives

  • Identify peak bike usage periods
  • Analyze customer riding behavior
  • Discover high-demand stations
  • Understand weekday vs weekend usage trends
  • Provide operational recommendations

🛠 Tools Used

Tool Purpose
Tableau Dashboard visualization
SQL Query Databases
Excel Data cleaning
CSV Dataset Source data

🔍 Key Insights

🚴 Peak Commuting Hours

Bike usage consistently peaks during weekday morning and evening commuting hours.

📈 Weekend Rider Behavior

rides tend to have longer durations, suggesting more leisure-oriented customer behavior.

📍 High-Demand Stations

Several stations consistently experience higher traffic volume, indicating potential opportunities for

inventory optimization.

📅 Seasonal Usage Patterns

Ride frequency increases significantly during warmer months, highlighting seasonal demand fluctuations.

💡 Business Recommendations

  • Increase bike inventory during rush hours
  • Optimize station rebalancing strategies
  • Expand capacity at high-demand stations
  • Launch weekend-focused marketing campaigns
  • Improve maintenance scheduling during peak seasons
